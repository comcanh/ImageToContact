# Privacy Policy for Add contact from Image

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Add contact from Image** ("we", "our", or "app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is processed.

---

### 1. Information We Access and How We Use It

Our application is designed to help users quickly extract text from photos and create phone contacts directly on their device. To provide this functionality, the app requests the following system permissions:

* **Photos and Media (Images):** 
  * The app accesses only the specific photo/image shared or selected by the user.
  * Images are analyzed locally on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition to detect phone numbers and contact names.
  * We **do not** upload, store, or transmit your images to any external server or third party.

* **Contacts (`READ_CONTACTS`, `WRITE_CONTACTS`):**
  * Used solely to pre-fill the system contact creation form and allow you to save the extracted name, phone number, and photo avatar into your address book.
  * Your contacts data remains entirely on your device (or synced to your own connected Google Account per your Android settings). We do not collect, read, or sell your contact list.

---

### 2. On-Device Processing

All Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed **100% on-device (offline)** using Google ML Kit. No facial recognition, biometric data, or private document details are collected, transferred, or processed outside of your smartphone.

---

The free version of our app may display advertisements provided by third-party services. These third-party services may collect standard advertising identifiers and diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

* **Google AdMob:** Used to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and app interaction data for ad customization and fraud prevention.  
  Read Google's Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Google Play In-App Billing:** Handles secure in-app purchases (such as upgrading to remove ads). Financial and payment transactions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not store or have access to your credit card or payment information.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
